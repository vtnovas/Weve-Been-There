Who Are You?

My husband, Victor, and I went through the family-based green card process after we got married. Somewhere between the paperwork, the waiting, and the questions, I started keeping track of what we were learning.

There is a lot of correct information out there. What's harder to find is someone explaining it in casual language. I kept wishing for something that told me not just what we needed to submit, but what to expect along the way. What's normal to feel confused about? Where do delays tend to happen? What is actually in our control?

This guide is what I wished we had. It's shaped by our own timeline, but also by the experiences of people across the r/USCIS community. I made this for those filing without a lawyer, juggling full lives, and/or figuring it out one document at a time.

I'm not an attorney, and this isn't legal advice. I'm only someone who has been through the process, trying to build the resource I couldn't find.

What Is This?

"We've Been There" is a guide to the family-based green card process, built for the everyday person going through it.

Each chapter follows a stage of the journey, in the order we moved through it: from planning your route, to gathering evidence, to the long wait, and beyond. You don't have to read it front to back, and there are many correct orders to go in depending on your own situation. If you know where you are in the process, jump straight to that chapter. If you're just starting out, Plan Your Route is the best place to begin.
